Manifesto · 001

A frame is an argument

I don't believe in neutral images. Every exposure decision is a claim about what deserves attention and what can be allowed to fall away into the dark. The grade is where that claim becomes undeniable.

So I work backwards from the argument. Lens, movement, palette, cut — none of it is style for its own sake. If a choice can't defend itself in the edit, it leaves. What survives is the only thing worth showing you.

Make it specific or don't make it.
